🏘️ Use Cases

Built for every
research context

From household welfare surveys to clinical trials — FieldGovern is built for the full spectrum of India's field research and monitoring needs.

🏘️

Household Surveys

NGOs · Government Welfare Programs

Collect household-level data across thousands of respondents with GPS-tagged interviews, skip-logic branching based on household composition, and offline-first reliability in areas with no connectivity.

🏥

Clinical Data Collection

Health Researchers · ICMR Studies

Capture clinical measurements, patient consent, and diagnostic observations with structured repeat groups and DPDP-compliant data handling. Purpose field and audit trail built in for IRB compliance.

🌾

Agricultural Census

State Governments · Agri-Research Institutes

Map plots via GPS, capture crop type, area, and input usage with barcode support for package scanning. Works in remote fields with zero mobile internet — syncs when enumerators return to range.

🏫

Education Monitoring

School Inspection · Attendance Tracking

Conduct school visits, infrastructure audits, and attendance spot-checks using photo evidence and GPS verification. Supervisors get real-time alerts when inspectors complete a school round.

🔬

Impact Evaluation

RCTs · J-PAL Style Studies · DIME

Rigorous double-blind data collection with idempotent submission IDs, back-check audit modules, and Stata .dta export ready for analysis. Designed around the J-PAL and DIME field protocols.

🛒

Market Research

Consumer Insights · Brand Tracking

Run brand tracking surveys, product intercept interviews, and consumer perception studies. Google Sheets live sync means your research team sees results as data arrives from the field.
