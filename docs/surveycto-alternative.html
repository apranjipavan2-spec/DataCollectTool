Where SurveyCTO wins

Let's be honest about it. SurveyCTO has been around since 2010 and built deep credibility with the World Bank, IPA, J-PAL, and the global RCT community. If any of the following describe you, stay on SurveyCTO — for now:

  • You run multi-country, RCT-grade research where your funder mandates SurveyCTO
  • You depend on Stata-side workflows (server-side calculations, SCTO bridges) that need IPA's exact stack
  • You have hundreds of complex XLSForms with custom appearance widgets that took years to build
  • You pay in USD and the cost ratio doesn't matter to you

Where FieldGovern wins

1. Price — built around Indian budgets

SurveyCTO's smallest paid plan is roughly $165/month, billed in USD with a foreign currency markup of 2–4% on most Indian cards, plus the FX hit. FieldGovern's Starter plan is ₹6,999/month — and you can pay by UPI, Net Banking, or any Indian card. For a 12-person enumeration team, the total cost difference over a year is usually ₹1.5–2.5 lakhs.

2. AI tooling SurveyCTO simply doesn't ship

FieldGovern is built on a Claude/GPT-powered analysis stack. The four AI capabilities above typically save a research team 8–20 hours per project — which is more than the entire SurveyCTO bill.

3. India-first by default

FieldGovern's UI ships in English, Hindi, Kannada, and Telugu. Data hosting defaults to Indian data centres. The platform is DPDP Act 2023 compliant by design (encryption at rest and in transit, audit trails, RBAC, tenant isolation). On SurveyCTO, India hosting is an add-on and there is no localised UI.

4. Modern, no-install PWA

SurveyCTO Collect is an Android-only APK that field staff need to sideload or install from Play Store, then keep updated. FieldGovern is a Progressive Web App — enumerators open a URL in Chrome on Android (or Safari on iOS), tap "Add to Home Screen", and it works fully offline. No store approvals, no APK distribution, no version-fragmentation headaches.

5. UPI billing and Indian invoicing

Pay by UPI, NetBanking, or Indian credit card. GST-compliant invoices in INR. No need to expense USD subscriptions or chase finance teams for international card approvals every quarter.

Frequently asked questions

Is FieldGovern really cheaper than SurveyCTO?

Yes. FieldGovern starts at roughly one third the cost of SurveyCTO's Team plan and is billed in INR with UPI support — no FX markup or international card fees. Over a year, most Indian teams save ₹1.5–2.5 lakhs.

Does FieldGovern work offline like SurveyCTO?

Yes. FieldGovern is a true offline-first PWA. Enumerators can collect forms, photos, audio, and GPS without internet; everything syncs automatically when a connection returns. Auto-save runs every 300ms locally.

Can I migrate my SurveyCTO forms to FieldGovern?

Yes. FieldGovern supports CSV import of submissions and a visual form builder that mirrors XLSForm logic, so existing instruments can usually be recreated in under an hour each. Our onboarding team will help during your first week.

Is FieldGovern DPDP 2023 compliant?

Yes. Data is hosted in India, encrypted at rest and in transit, with role-based access control, full audit trails, and tenant isolation — all designed against India's DPDP Act 2023.

What about AI features?

FieldGovern ships four AI tools SurveyCTO does not: AI form builder, AI data cleaning, AI cross-tab interpretation, and AI report writing in your preferred narrative style. You can use OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini under the hood — your choice, your API key, your data.

Does FieldGovern support Stata and SPSS exports?

Yes. Stata (.dta) export is on all plans. SPSS export is on Research and higher plans.

Can I pay by UPI?

Yes. UPI, NetBanking, and any Indian credit / debit card. GST-compliant invoices in INR.
