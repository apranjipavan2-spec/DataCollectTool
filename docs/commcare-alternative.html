Where CommCare wins

CommCare is genuinely best-in-class for certain workloads. Stay on CommCare if:

  • You're running clinical case management. Mother / child tracking with longitudinal medical records, ICD-10 coding, EHR-style workflows — CommCare's depth in this vertical is unmatched.
  • You need integration with state HMIS / DHIS2 with clinical-grade fidelity. CommCare has 15+ years of these integrations in production.
  • Your funder (USAID, BMGF, WHO) requires CommCare specifically. Some grants mandate it.
  • You operate at very large scale (10,000+ field health workers) with case-load complexity that benefits from enterprise-grade tooling.
  • You need their global research-grade case-management methodology. Dimagi has done a lot of methodological work the rest of us haven't.

Where FieldGovern wins

1. Transparent self-serve pricing

CommCare is sold via enterprise contracts: discovery calls, scoping documents, multi-week sales cycles, opaque pricing that typically lands in the lakhs-per-year range. FieldGovern publishes its pricing on the website. You can sign up, pay by UPI, and be collecting in under 10 minutes. For programs under 50 users, FieldGovern is often an order of magnitude cheaper.

2. AI analysis tooling CommCare doesn't ship

CommCare is excellent at the collection half of the workflow. It doesn't help much with analysis. FieldGovern ships four AI tools designed for what happens after the data lands:

  • AI form builder. Describe a survey; get a draft in 30 seconds.
  • AI data cleaning (FgCleaner). Outlier detection, free-text harmonisation, recoding rules.
  • AI cross-tab interpretation (FgAnalyzer). Written narratives for every cross-tab in your preferred style.
  • AI report writing (FgWriter). Publication-ready report drafts in your organisation's voice.

For program teams already paying for analysts and consultants to clean data and write reports, the time savings are substantial — often 8–20 hours per project.

3. Modern PWA — no APK distribution

CommCare's collection app is an Android APK that field workers need to install, keep updated, and troubleshoot. FieldGovern is a Progressive Web App — Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS), tap "Add to Home Screen", and it works fully offline. No store approvals, no version-fragmentation, no IT-department dependency.

4. India-first by default

UI in English, Hindi, Kannada, and Telugu. Indian hosting. DPDP 2023 compliance. INR / UPI billing with GST-compliant invoices. CommCare can do most of this on enterprise tiers; FieldGovern does it out of the box on every plan.

5. Faster iteration cycle

CommCare's form-revision workflow is robust but heavy — version management, app publishing, distribution. FieldGovern's web-based forms update instantly: edit, save, the next sync picks up the new version. For pilot work and rapid iteration, this difference matters.

Migration is straightforward (for most workflows)

Migrating survey and tracking workloads from CommCare to FieldGovern is straightforward. Migrating deep clinical case-management workloads is a bigger lift and we'll be honest with you during scoping if your use-case doesn't fit yet.

For most non-clinical migrations:

  • Day 1: Export your CommCare form data as Excel / CSV. Import into FieldGovern.
  • Day 2–4: Recreate your top forms using the FieldGovern visual builder or AI form builder.
  • Day 5–7: Pilot with a small team side-by-side with CommCare.
  • Week 2–3: Roll out, retire CommCare at next contract renewal.

Our onboarding team will scope your migration for free before you commit. If we don't fit yet, we'll tell you.
