Where Qualtrics wins

To be clear, Qualtrics is impressive software. Stay on Qualtrics if:

  • You run a real XM program. Brand tracking with millions of touchpoints, EX programs across thousands of employees, X-data + O-data correlations. Qualtrics is purpose-built for this and FieldGovern is not.
  • You're already deeply integrated. Pre-built XM dashboards, Salesforce data connectors, multi-year template investment — switching has real cost.
  • You need the panel marketplace. Qualtrics's audience network is one of the largest globally.
  • Your CFO is fine with the spend. If Qualtrics is well under 0.5% of your operating budget, the question may be moot.

Where FieldGovern wins

1. Pricing that matches Indian budgets

Qualtrics's $1,500–3,000/user/year USD is roughly ₹1.2–2.5 lakhs per user per year. A 10-user team is ₹12–25 lakhs annually. FieldGovern's Corporate plan at ₹39,999/month is ₹4.8 lakhs annually for the whole organisation — 80–95% cheaper, in INR.

2. Offline-first by design

Qualtrics has an Offline Surveys app but the experience is bolted-on. FieldGovern is offline-first from the architecture up — same instrument online or offline, no separate app, no separate sync flow.

3. India-hosted and DPDP-aligned

Qualtrics's default hosting is US / EU. Configuring India-resident storage and a DPDP-aligned DPA is possible but takes negotiation and adds cost. FieldGovern is India-hosted by default with a standard DPA.

4. AI cleaning / cross-tab / report writing included

Qualtrics charges premium add-ons (TextIQ, StatsIQ, premium XM modules) for the AI capabilities. FieldGovern's FgCleaner / FgAnalyzer / FgWriter are included on the standard plans.

5. India-hours support

Qualtrics's support team is global; India-timezone response can be slow. FieldGovern's team is in India.

6. Multilingual enumerator UI

Full Hindi / Kannada / Telugu enumerator UI — not just translated form labels.

Migration is straightforward (mostly)

  • Day 1–2: Export Qualtrics survey responses as CSV / SPSS. Document survey logic from the Qualtrics builder.
  • Day 3–5: Recreate top 1–3 active surveys in FieldGovern's visual or AI builder. Complex piping / display logic may take iteration.
  • Day 6–7: Pilot side-by-side; cut over once parity confirmed.
  • Ongoing: If you use Qualtrics's brand-tracking / EX modules, plan that workstream separately — those don't map 1:1 to FieldGovern.

Who should switch

  • Indian mid-market enterprises paying Qualtrics for what is functionally a survey + reporting tool.
  • Research firms running CAPI or CATI plus online surveys, where Qualtrics's premium isn't earned.
  • NGOs and government programs handed Qualtrics by a donor and renewing reluctantly.
  • Any team where DPDP compliance, INR billing, or offline collection is becoming a constraint.
