Use Cases

Built for every
research & survey type

Household, socio-economic and M&E surveys · baseline/midline/endline · impact evaluation & RCTs · KAP & public health · agriculture, education, WASH & livelihoods · census enumeration · panel & longitudinal studies · CAPI/CATI · social audits and market research. FieldGovern handles them all.

Built with Dataworx · 5 projects delivered · used on fieldwork for a Karnataka State M&E authority

Household Surveys

NGOs · Government Welfare Programs

Collect household-level data across thousands of respondents with GPS-tagged interviews, skip-logic branching based on household composition, and offline-first reliability in areas with no connectivity.

Clinical Data Collection

Health Researchers · ICMR Studies

Capture clinical measurements, patient consent, and diagnostic observations with structured repeat groups and DPDP-compliant data handling. Purpose field and audit trail built in for IRB compliance.

Agricultural Census

State Governments · Agri-Research Institutes

Map plots via GPS, capture crop type, area, and input usage with barcode support for package scanning. Works in remote fields with zero mobile internet — syncs when enumerators return to range.

Education Monitoring

School Inspection · Attendance Tracking

Conduct school visits, infrastructure audits, and attendance spot-checks using photo evidence and GPS verification. Supervisors get real-time alerts when inspectors complete a school round.

Impact Evaluation

RCTs · J-PAL Style Studies · DIME

Rigorous double-blind data collection with idempotent submission IDs, back-check audit modules, and Stata .dta export ready for analysis. Designed around the J-PAL and DIME field protocols.

Market Research

Consumer Insights · Brand Tracking

Run brand tracking surveys, product intercept interviews, and consumer perception studies. Google Sheets live sync means your research team sees results as data arrives from the field.

Baseline · Midline · Endline

M&E Teams · Donor-funded Programs

Run multi-round evaluations on the same instrument with versioned questionnaires, wave tracking, and comparison-ready exports — purpose-built for baseline–midline–endline study designs.

Panel & Longitudinal Studies

Cohort Tracking · Attrition Analysis

Track the same respondents across waves with built-in panel-study tooling and attrition reports — follow individuals and households over months or years without losing the thread.

Public Health & Nutrition

KAP · DHS-style · Anthropometry

Knowledge-Attitude-Practice (KAP) surveys, nutrition and anthropometric measurement, and DHS-style health indicators with validation, consent capture, and DPDP-compliant handling.

WASH & Livelihoods

Water · Sanitation · SHG Programs

Facility audits, water-quality and sanitation surveys, and livelihoods / self-help-group assessments with GPS, photo evidence, and offline collection in the most remote locations.

Government Schemes & Social Audits

Scheme Monitoring · Governance · Beneficiary Tracking

Monitor scheme implementation, run social audits, and track beneficiaries across districts, blocks and gram panchayats with hierarchy-aware roles and real-time field monitoring.

CATI & Phone Surveys

Remote Interviewing · Rapid Assessments

Run computer-assisted telephone interviews (CATI) and rapid phone-based assessments alongside in-person CAPI — same form, same dashboard, whether the interview is face-to-face or remote.

Census & Large Enumeration

State-scale Counts · Listing Operations

Run large-scale enumeration and listing operations with location targets, progress dashboards by area, and reliable offline sync across hundreds of enumerators.
