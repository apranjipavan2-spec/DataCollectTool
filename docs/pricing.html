Frequently asked questions

How is FieldGovern different from KoboToolbox?

KoboToolbox collects data — FieldGovern collects and analyses it. Every paid plan includes AI Data Cleaning (with audit trail), FG Writer for donor reports, Smart Builder for cross-tabs, and panel study tracking. On KoboToolbox these require a separate data analyst costing ₹30,000–₹50,000/month. FieldGovern includes them from ₹6,999/month.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Your 30-day free trial starts the moment your organisation is created. No payment details required. Activate a paid plan at any time by UPI or bank transfer.

How do I pay?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and direct bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS). Our admin confirms payment and activates your plan within 2–4 business hours.

Can I switch plans mid-cycle?

Yes. Contact us and we will upgrade or downgrade your plan. Upgrades are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

What happens when I hit the submission limit?

New submissions are blocked and you receive an in-app notification. All existing data remains safe. Upgrade anytime to continue collecting.

Who qualifies for the Free plan?

The Free plan is open to everyone — students, NGOs, researchers, government teams and companies alike. No card, no credit check: just sign up. It's free forever, with 1,000 submissions/month, offline collection, GPS validation and consent capture. Need more volume or the full AI suite? Move to a paid or Custom plan anytime.

How does Custom / metered pricing work?

If you don't fit a standard plan, we price it your way — by total form submissions, by number of active schedules / forms, or per project / study. Custom includes every enterprise capability (SSO, on-premise/DPDP deployment, white-label, multi-org dashboard, dedicated CSM). Message us on WhatsApp with your scope and we'll send a quote.

Is the introductory discount permanent?

No — the struck-through price is our standard rate and the discounted price is a limited-time introductory offer. Lock in the intro rate now and, on annual or 3-year billing, your rate is held for the full term.

Can I get a demo before buying?

Yes — try our live demo at fieldgovern.com/demo with no signup. For a personalised walkthrough, message us on WhatsApp and we'll schedule a live demo for your team.
