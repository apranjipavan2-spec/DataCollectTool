Frequently asked questions

How is FieldGovern different from KoboToolbox?

KoboToolbox collects data — FieldGovern collects and analyses it. Every paid plan includes AI Data Cleaning (with audit trail), FG Writer for donor reports, Smart Builder for cross-tabs, and panel study tracking. On KoboToolbox these require a separate data analyst costing ₹30,000–₹50,000/month. FieldGovern includes them from ₹6,499/month.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Your 30-day free trial starts the moment your organisation is created. No payment details required. Activate a paid plan at any time by UPI or bank transfer.

How do I pay?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and direct bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS). Our admin confirms payment and activates your plan within 2–4 business hours.

Can I switch plans mid-cycle?

Yes. Contact us and we will upgrade or downgrade your plan. Upgrades are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

What happens when I hit the submission limit?

New submissions are blocked and you receive an in-app notification. All existing data remains safe. Upgrade anytime to continue collecting.

Is there a discount for NGOs?

The NGO segment already carries our lowest pricing — up to 20% below comparable KoboToolbox plans. Additional goodwill discounts for very small NGOs can be arranged — contact us on WhatsApp.

Can I get a demo before buying?

Yes — try our live demo at fieldgovern.com/demo with no signup. For a personalised walkthrough, message us on WhatsApp and we'll schedule a live demo for your team.
