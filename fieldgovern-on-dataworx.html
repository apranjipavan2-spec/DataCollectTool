The Problem

What field research teams are actually up against

Every Dataworx engagement runs into the same operational friction — this is what FieldGovern was built to remove.

Connectivity you can't rely on

TRAI data puts roughly a quarter of rural India on 2G or no signal at all. Tools built assuming Wi-Fi lose submissions, corrupt syncs, or simply won't load — right when an enumerator is mid-interview.

Foreign pricing, Indian budgets

USD-denominated licensing eats disproportionately into INR grant budgets, with no local GST invoicing and support hours that rarely overlap IST.

DPDP compliance as an afterthought

Most survey tools weren't built with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act in mind. Consent capture, retention schedules, and audit trails get bolted on late — or not at all.

The gap between data and report

Raw submissions still have to be cleaned, tabulated, interpreted, and written up — usually by hand in Excel, usually by someone without a dedicated data-analyst title.

The Platform

One platform, from first interview to final report

Everything a Dataworx-led study needs, in a single offline-capable tool.

True offline collection

Enumerators fill forms with zero internet on low-end Android devices. Submissions save locally and sync automatically the moment a signal returns.

AI form builder

Describe a study's objectives in plain language and get a complete form back — questions, validation, and skip logic included.

FG Analyzer

AI-suggested cross-tabulations and charts from submitted data, plus purpose-built panel-study attrition reporting.

FG Cleaner

Surfaces duplicates, outliers, missing-GPS records, and rushed submissions before they ever reach analysis.

FG Writer

Generates a publication-ready draft report from your tables — styled for a Government, NGO/Donor, or Research audience.

DPDP-native compliance

Timestamped consent capture, audit logs, and a signed Data Processing Agreement — built in, not bolted on.

Live field map

GPS-plotted submissions with geofencing, so supervisors can spot coverage gaps and flagged clusters in real time.

Indian-language forms

One-click translation of form labels into Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, and more — for enumerators and respondents alike.

Analysis-ready exports

Clean exports to CSV, Stata (.dta), SPSS, and Google Sheets — no manual reformatting before it lands in your stats package.
