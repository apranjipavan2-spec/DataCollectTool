Overall ranking for Indian field teams

Scored on offline reliability, built-in analysis & AI, India-first features (languages, DPDP, UPI) and total cost of ownership. Each tool is excellent at something — here's where they win.

FieldGovern BEST OVERALL · INDIA
Best for: end-to-end field research in India — collect, clean, analyse and report in one place. Offline PWA (no install), AI data cleaning, cross-tabs, panel study, FG Writer reports, DPDP compliance, UPI billing, 22 Indian languages. Lowest total cost.
