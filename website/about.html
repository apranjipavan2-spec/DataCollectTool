🏛️ Our Story

Built by researchers,
for researchers.

FieldGovern was born from firsthand frustration with expensive, unreliable tools that break in the field. We set out to build the platform India's research teams deserve — affordable, offline-first, and natively India-ready.

World-class data tools at Indian prices

India's field researchers collect some of the most important data in the world under the most challenging conditions. Yet they've been forced to choose between expensive Western platforms or unreliable homegrown spreadsheets.

FieldGovern changes that. At a fraction of the cost of legacy systems, we're engineered specifically for Indian field environments: low-end Android devices, spotty 2G connectivity, regional language support, and local research workflows.

Our platform works completely offline. Data is stored securely on the local device and synced the moment connectivity returns — zero data loss, guaranteed.
