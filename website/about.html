🏛️ About FieldGovern

Built by researchers,
for researchers

FieldGovern was born from firsthand frustration with expensive, unreliable tools that break in the field. We set out to build the platform India's research teams deserve — affordable, offline-first, and India-ready.

🎯 Our Mission

World-class data tools at Indian prices

India's field researchers deserve tools built for Indian conditions — not expensive foreign platforms that assume 4G connectivity and ample budget.

India's field researchers — public health workers, agricultural scientists, census enumerators, NGO field teams — collect some of the most important data in the world under the most challenging conditions. Yet they've been forced to choose between expensive Western platforms or unreliable homegrown spreadsheets.

FieldGovern changes that. At ₹18,000/month for unlimited users vs SurveyCTO's $630/month, we're not just cheaper — we're built specifically for Indian field conditions: low-end Android devices, spotty 2G connectivity, regional language support, and local research workflows.

Our platform works completely offline. Data is stored locally on the device and synced the moment connectivity returns — no data loss, ever. Whether your team is in a remote tribal area in Odisha or a semi-urban block in Karnataka, FieldGovern keeps working.

We support Hindi, Kannada, and Telugu natively, with more languages being added based on researcher demand. Our form builder is flexible enough for complex public health surveys, agricultural assessments, and government program monitoring alike.

🇮🇳

India-first design

Built for Indian network conditions, Indian languages, Indian research workflows, and Indian budget constraints. Not a port of a Western tool.

🔒

Data sovereignty

Your data stays in your tenant. Multi-tenant isolation enforced at the database layer — not just application policy. Row-Level Security on every query.

⚡

Open and extensible

REST API, webhooks, and CSV/Stata/Sheets export. Integrate with your existing research pipeline without vendor lock-in.

📵

True offline-first

Collect data in zero-connectivity conditions. Data syncs automatically when you're back online. No lost submissions, ever.

🤝 Strategic Partnership

Powered by Dataworx expertise

FieldGovern is developed in strategic partnership with Dataworx — a data science and research analytics firm with deep roots in India's public sector and development research ecosystem.

📊
Strategic Growth Partner

Dataworx

Dataworx is a Bengaluru-based data science and analytics firm specializing in research data management, program monitoring & evaluation (M&E), and field survey technology for government and development sector clients across India.

As FieldGovern's strategic growth partner, Dataworx brings deep domain expertise in how India's research teams actually work in the field — the challenges they face, the data they need to collect, and the analytical workflows they rely on. This partnership ensures that FieldGovern's product roadmap stays grounded in real-world Indian research needs, not abstract assumptions.

Dataworx's network of researchers, program officers, and field coordinators across multiple states provides ongoing feedback that shapes every feature we build — from the form builder's skip logic to the export formats supported for Stata and SPSS analysis.

Led by Manjunath YN (CEO, Dataworx) — a seasoned researcher and program evaluation specialist who brings over a decade of experience in large-scale field data collection and M&E for government and civil society programs across India.
