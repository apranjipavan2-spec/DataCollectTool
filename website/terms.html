1. The Service

FieldGovern provides a B2B software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform for offline-first field data collection, monitoring & evaluation (M&E), and research data management. The service is accessible via:

  • A web application hosted at app.fieldgovern.com
  • A Progressive Web App (PWA) installable on Android and iOS mobile devices
  • A REST API for programmatic integration with your organization's systems

FieldGovern is designed for India's research and development sector, including public health researchers, agricultural scientists, census teams, NGO field staff, and government program monitoring units. The platform's core capability is the ability to collect and store data completely offline, syncing to the central server when connectivity is restored.

The platform is developed by Pavankumar Deshetty (Research Associate & Data Analyst, Kalike — Tata Trusts, Bengaluru) with the strategic guidance and domain expertise of Manjunath YN (CEO, Dataworx, Bengaluru) who serves as strategic growth partner.
