How to actually pick

  • Constraint = software budget. Start with KoboToolbox or ODK.
  • Constraint = compliance. India-hosted DPDP-aligned options: FieldGovern, or self-hosted Kobo/ODK on Indian infra.
  • Constraint = analyst time. Tools with AI cleaning + analysis save 8–20 hours per project. Today, FieldGovern is the only one in India shipping that natively.
  • Constraint = enumerator training time. Multilingual UI matters. FieldGovern, Kobo, and CommCare lead here; FieldGovern is strongest on the Indian-language UX.
  • Constraint = donor / procurement requirements. India-hosted + DPA + audit trail. FieldGovern is built for this profile.
  • Constraint = scale of global team. If your team is global and English-speaking, SurveyCTO's ecosystem is hard to beat.

Frequently asked questions

Which survey app is best for Indian NGOs?

For zero-budget projects, KoboToolbox. For NGOs with some budget who want AI tools and DPDP compliance, FieldGovern. For health programs, CommCare.

Which survey app is best for offline fieldwork in India?

KoboToolbox, ODK Collect, SurveyCTO, and FieldGovern. All work offline; FieldGovern is the only one without an APK distribution overhead (PWA, works on Android + iOS).

Which survey app is best for market research in India?

SurveyCTO and FieldGovern dominate the Indian market-research CAPI category. FieldGovern wins on price, AI analysis, and Indian-language UI; SurveyCTO wins on global track record.

Are Google Forms or SurveyMonkey good enough for serious research?

Not for field research. No offline, no GPS, no back-check workflow, no panel-study management. Fine for ad-hoc polls.

Is FieldGovern open source?

No. FieldGovern is commercial. The open-source alternatives are KoboToolbox (AGPLv3) and ODK (Apache 2.0).

How is the ranking ordered?

By fit for the Indian buyer in 2026, not by global market share. We weight India hosting, INR/UPI billing, multilingual UI, DPDP compliance, and AI tooling heavily because that's what our buyers ask for.
