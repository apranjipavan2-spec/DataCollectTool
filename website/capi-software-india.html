The Best CAPI Software for India (2026)

Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI) is the workhorse of Indian market and social research — IMRB, Hansa, AC Nielsen, NSSO, NCAER, every serious M&E shop runs CAPI fieldwork. The category is well established; what is not well established is a tool that combines first-class CAPI rigour (back-checks, GPS verification, panel-study management, validation) with modern offline-first reliability, Indian-language UIs, AI analysis, and INR pricing. FieldGovern is built for that exact intersection.

What CAPI software needs to do, in practice

  • Render the instrument flawlessly. Skip logic, constraint rules, calculated fields, repeat groups, randomisation — without crashing mid-interview.
  • Capture rich evidence. Photos of forms, audio of consent, GPS at submit.
  • Run offline for days. Assignment download once on Wi-Fi, days of fieldwork, sync on reconnect.
  • Support multi-language interviewing. Hindi-speaking respondent, English-reading supervisor — same form, different surfaces.
  • Enforce data quality at the device. Validation rules, soft-warns, hard-stops, geofences.
  • Enable back-checks. 10–20% of submissions re-interviewed by an independent enumerator. Discrepancy report feeds enumerator scorecards.
  • Manage panel studies. Track respondents across waves, monitor attrition.
  • Move data to analysis cleanly. Stata / SPSS / CSV exports with labels intact.

The CAPI workflow on FieldGovern

1. Build the instrument

Drag-and-drop builder for fast authoring. Or describe the questionnaire in plain English to the AI form builder and iterate on the draft. Add skip rules and constraints with click-and-pick; preview as the enumerator would see it.

2. Translate

One-click AI translation of labels into Hindi / Kannada / Telugu / English. Review and edit translations inline.

3. Assign and download

Supervisor assigns the form to enumerators with target counts and (optionally) geofences. Enumerators sync once on Wi-Fi; form, choice lists, and assignment now live on the device.

4. Collect — offline, multi-day

Enumerator runs interviews with full skip logic, validation, GPS watching, photo and audio capture. Auto-save every 300ms. Submission queue persists across phone restarts.

5. Sync

When the device is back online, the service worker syncs in the background. Geofence violations, duplicates, and validation soft-warns are flagged at sync time.

6. Quality control

Supervisor reviews submissions in the dashboard. Flag 10–20% for back-check; the back-check form (a subset of the original) is assigned to a second enumerator. Discrepancy reports feed the enumerator scorecard.

7. Analysis and reporting

Pipe submissions into FgCleaner (auto-flag outliers, harmonise categories), then FgAnalyzer (cross-tabs with AI-written interpretation), then FgWriter (publication-ready report draft). Or export Stata / SPSS / CSV for your own pipeline.

Frequently asked questions

What is CAPI software?

Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing. Software used by enumerators to conduct face-to-face surveys on a tablet or phone, with skip logic, validation, multimedia capture, GPS, and central sync.

Does FieldGovern support back-check workflows?

Yes. Flag a submission for back-check, assign a back-check form to a second enumerator, compare responses, feed accuracy into the enumerator scorecard.

Can FieldGovern verify the interview happened at the right location?

Yes. GPS at submit, geofence enforcement per assignment, background GPS watching for best-accuracy fix.

Does FieldGovern handle panel studies?

Yes. Wave numbers, wave labels, attrition reporting with lost-ID list.

Can the interview language be different from the supervisor language?

Yes. Multi-language form labels; enumerator sees the respondent's language, supervisor sees their own.

Does the data export work with Stata and SPSS?

Yes. Native .dta and .sav exports with labels intact.
