🚀 Platform Features

Everything your field team needs
— in one platform

From offline data collection to AI-generated reports — every tool your field research team requires, built for India's connectivity realities.

Start Free Trial →
📱

Offline-First Collection

Works without internet — forms are cached locally so enumerators can collect data anywhere, from remote villages to basement clinics. Data auto-syncs securely the moment a connection returns, with zero data loss guaranteed.

🏗️

Drag-and-Drop Form Builder

Design complex surveys visually with 14+ field types, advanced AND/OR skip logic, form versioning, and repeat groups. No coding required — what you build is exactly what enumerators see in the field.

🔄

Multi-Platform Import

Migrate from XLSForm, KoboToolbox, SurveyCTO, or ODK Central with a single click — your existing forms come in exactly as designed. No manual re-entry, no lost skip logic.

🌐

Real-time Sync & Media

Photos, GPS coordinates, and audio recordings sync securely using a two-phase approach — text first on 2G, then media in background batches. 5 MB photos compress to ~200 KB automatically.

👁️

Supervisor Dashboard

Flag, approve, or reject individual submissions in real time, with full GPS map view and audit trail. Export any subset of data at any time as CSV, Stata .dta, or straight to Google Sheets.

🟢

WhatsApp Notifications

Instant MSG91 WABA alerts sent to supervisors when enumerators sync, flag, or complete their assignment. Stay informed on field events without refreshing a dashboard.

📊

Google Sheets Live Sync

Every submission is auto-appended as a new row to a connected Google Sheet the moment it syncs. Your data is always up to date without any manual exports or scheduled jobs.

🤖

AI Report Writer

Connect OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or Google Gemini using your own API key. Generate professional 3–5 paragraph markdown summaries of any form's submissions in seconds.

✅

QC Suite

Server-side validation rules, automated duplicate detection using idempotency keys, and a built-in back-check audit workflow ensure your data is clean before analysis begins.

🛡️

DPDP Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 is built into the platform — consent tracking per respondent, full audit logs, a data erasure endpoint, and a purpose field on every form.

🌍

Multilingual

English, Hindi, Kannada, and Telugu are available out of the box for the enumerator interface. AI-powered label translation lets you add new languages to any form in minutes.
