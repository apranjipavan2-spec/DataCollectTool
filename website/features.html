🔨 Smart Builder

Build tables in two ways.
Both powered by AI.

Column Picker: Search and select specific columns from your dataset. AI sees the real unique values from your submissions, then designs the optimal table structure — groupby, aggregation, chart type, and pre-cleaned column labels in one shot.

Ask a Question: Type in plain English — "average age of beneficiaries per district, broken down by gender" — and AI parses all your column headers to build the full table specification automatically.

  • ✓Column labels pre-cleaned by AI before the table is built
  • ✓NL query understands field research intent — not just keywords
  • ✓All tables land in the same shared store — instantly visible in Writer
